## What was done

1. Pre-registration and test-set guide committed first (`data/qa/2026-10-13/`).
2. Dev set of 286 bilingual questions written (agent), used only for development.
3. D0 answerer, tests, ADR-035.

## What was found

- The existing verifier rejected 166 of 432 first-draft answer/situation/language combinations; every rejection was an English sentence opener (or a banned word such as "it works") that rules 2 and 6 cannot ground. Rewording fixed all; the verifier was not changed.
- The cue lists reach 100% on the dev set only because they were adjusted on it. That figure is not a result.

## 15 October: what "satisfactory" means for run1, written before any evaluation result exists

The owner is away and asked whether the result is satisfactory. These are the readings fixed in advance; they say whether to **continue** (export, quantise, blind ratings, phone test), never whether to **adopt** (that needs the pre-registered sealed test set, blind ratings, faithfulness audit and phone ceilings).

- **Understanding (task U), 300 validation questions:** intent accuracy at least 0.90 overall and at least 0.85 on the Tamil questions; unparseable replies at most 2%. The validation questions come from the same bank as the training questions, so even a pass overstates generalisation.
- **Writing (task W), 60 validation answers, given the gold intents:** at least 90% pass the verifier; **every** safety question is refused; **every** low- or stale-confidence answer carries the hedge. The reference is the untouched base model (0 of 10 passed).
- **Reading:** the first 12 answers are read by the coding agent for fluency and faithfulness (not blind; informal, noted as such).
- **If the readings are missed:** the result is reported as it is; the settings are not tuned and rerun (addendum 2). A second run is allowed only to fix a bug, and then both runs are reported.
- **If they are met:** "promising", nothing more. The model still has to beat D0 version 2 in the blind pairwise ratings by at least three team members before it is adopted.
