# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback graciously, and keep discussion about
the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not
tolerated. Critique of the science is welcome and expected; this project's own review is public in
`04_critique_and_review/`.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who do.
To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private GitHub message or the security advisory form
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
